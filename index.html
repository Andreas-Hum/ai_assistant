Tool argument check

About 30 minutes for 150 short questions. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can stop and continue later.

What you do

An AI assistant on smart glasses sees a situation and calls a tool, for example a web search or a calendar entry. The tool is always the right one. You only judge what it filled in: compare the model's answer with the reference answer, given the situation.

  • Correct would help the user just as well. Different words for the same thing count as correct.
  • Partial same topic, but asks for something different or narrower, or one of several fields is wrong.
  • Incorrect a different topic, or would not help the user.

⟨output of …⟩ means the value is taken from an earlier step, e.g. the user's GPS position.

Do not overthink it: go with your first impression. Keys 1 2 3 work too.

Your answers and the name you type below are sent automatically to a Google Form of the person who asked you (every 10 answers and when you finish), so there is nothing to send by hand. Nothing else is collected.
