# Tool argument check

A short labelling task: an AI helper in smart glasses does a small job (for example a web search or a calendar entry),
and you judge whether what it typed is just as useful as what a person wrote: **Yes**, **Partly** or **No**
(stored as correct, partial and incorrect).

Open the page: https://andreas-hum.github.io/ai_assistant/

Your progress is saved in your browser. Your answers and the name you type are sent automatically to a Google Form
(after every answer, and again when you come back); nothing else is collected. If sending fails, the page offers a download instead.
